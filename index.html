📖 HOW TO PLAY

⌨️

Movement

Desktop: Arrows/WASD.
Mobile: drag the joystick.

🔫

Shooting

Your ship auto-fires forward. Aim by moving into position.

🛡

Shield

Space / ⚡ button for a temporary shield that blocks one hit.

💰

Power-ups

Shield, Speed, Magnet and Extra Life drift down — fly into them.

☄️

Enemies

Asteroids, meteors, drones, hunters, bombers, mines and lasers — each sector mixes different threats.

🌌

Levels

Survive each sector's timer or objective to unlock the next.

👑

Bosses

Sectors 5 and 10 end in multi-phase boss fights. Watch for attack warnings.

🔥

Combo

Chain kills and pickups without getting hit to multiply your score.

👋 WELCOME, PILOT

  1. Move your ship with the joystick or arrow keys / WASD.
  2. Avoid dangerous objects — collisions cost a life.
  3. Your ship auto-fires at destructible enemies.
  4. Collect power-ups to gain an edge.
  5. Survive each sector to unlock the next.
  6. Complete sectors to unlock new sectors and ships.
